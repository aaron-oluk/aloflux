# Aloflux — Project Context for Claude Code

## Company Overview

Aloflux LLC is a US-registered technology company (Delaware) with engineering delivery based in Kampala, Uganda. The company offers custom software development, enterprise automation, AI-powered SaaS solutions, and digital commerce infrastructure for businesses across Africa and global markets, primarily targeting clients in the US and Europe.

The Uganda-based delivery team is a deliberate strategic advantage: English-proficient, highly skilled, and significantly more cost-efficient than Eastern European or Latin American alternatives, without sacrificing quality.

## Engagement Model

Aloflux structures client engagements in three tiers:

**Tier 1 — Discovery Sprint**
A paid 2-week scoping engagement. Delivers a technical proposal and architecture recommendation. Price range: $1,500 to $3,000. This qualifies serious buyers and funds scoping work.

**Tier 2 — Project-Based Development**
Fixed-scope builds with milestone-based payments structured as 30% upfront, 40% at midpoint delivery, 30% on completion. Scope reductions are preferred over rate cuts when negotiating. Typical range: $8,000 to $50,000.

**Tier 3 — Retainer / Managed Team**
Ongoing monthly development, maintenance, or automation management. Target: $3,000 to $10,000 per month per client. This is the primary recurring revenue model.
