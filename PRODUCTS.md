# Aloflux Commercial Products

This document covers commercial software products that Aloflux builds, maintains, and distributes under its own brand. These are distinct from client projects. The goal is to generate recurring subscription revenue independently of service delivery.

---

## Live Products

### 1. School Management System

**URL:** https://schoolms.aloflux.com/

**Status:** Live

A complete school administration platform designed for primary and secondary schools. Built with an offline-first architecture to handle low or intermittent internet connectivity, which is common in many markets we serve.

**Key modules:**

- Student enrolment and personal records
- Academic performance tracking and report generation
- Attendance management with absence alerts
- Timetable and room scheduling
- School fee billing and payment tracking
- Parent communication and notice board
- Teacher and staff management

**Revenue model:** Per-school subscription based on student count (monthly or annual). Onboarding fee for configuration and training.

**Target buyers:** Private primary schools, secondary schools, multi-campus groups, church-run schools, and education NGOs.

### 2. Versefountain

**URL:** https://versefountain.com
**Status:** Live

A publishing platform for writers, poets, and literary communities. Writers publish and monetise original work; readers discover and engage with it.

**Key features:**

- Clean writing and publishing editor
- Discovery feed with genre and tag filtering
- Reader engagement (reactions, comments, follows)
- Author profiles with public portfolios
- Reading lists and bookmarks
- Content monetisation for premium pieces

**Revenue model:** Freemium with a paid tier for premium publishing tools and content monetisation. Potential for institutional licensing to literary organisations, book clubs, and educational programs.

**Target buyers:** Independent writers and poets, literary magazines, university creative writing programs, book clubs, publishers building a digital presence.

## Products in Development

### 3. POS and Inventory Management System

**Status:** In development (client prototype exists, being productised)

A point of sale and stock management platform for retail shops, pharmacies, hardware stores, and small distributors. Designed to work offline with automatic sync when connectivity is restored.

**Key modules:**

- Sales recording and receipt generation
- Stock tracking with low-stock alerts
- Supplier and purchase order management
- Barcode and product catalogue management
- Daily and monthly sales reporting
- Multi-location stock visibility

**Revenue model:** Per-business subscription based on location count and user seats. Annual plan discount.

**Target buyers:** Retail shops, pharmacies, hardware stores, agro-input dealers, and small distributors in East Africa and similar markets. Also viable for diaspora-owned businesses in the US and UK that have supply chain operations.

### 4. SACCO and Credit Union Management Platform

**Status:** Planned

A member management and loan processing platform for savings and credit cooperatives (SACCOs) and credit unions.

**Key modules:**

- Member registration and account management
- Savings and shares tracking
- Loan applications, approvals, and disbursements
- Repayment schedules and tracking
- Dividend and interest calculations
- Financial reporting and audit trails

**Revenue model:** Tiered subscription based on number of active members. Setup and migration fee for existing cooperatives.

**Target buyers:** SACCOs, village savings groups scaling up, church-based cooperatives, teacher SACCOs, and credit unions across East Africa and the diaspora.

### 8. HR and Payroll Management System

**Status:** Planned

A human resources and payroll platform for SMEs that need to manage staff records, attendance, leave, and monthly payroll without using complex enterprise software.

**Key modules:**

- Employee records and document storage
- Leave management and approval workflows
- Attendance tracking (manual or biometric integration)
- Payroll calculation with statutory deductions (NSSF, PAYE, local equivalents)
- Payslip generation and distribution
- Appraisal and performance tracking

**Revenue model:** Monthly subscription based on employee headcount. Payroll processing add-on for companies that want managed payroll.

**Target buyers:** SMEs with 10 to 200 employees, NGOs managing local staff, schools managing teaching and non-teaching staff, and businesses replacing spreadsheet-based HR.

## Product Priority Recommendation

Based on market demand, existing client proximity, and revenue potential, the recommended build order is:

1. **POS and Inventory** (prototype already exists, clearest market, high transaction frequency drives retention)
2. **SACCO Platform** (underserved segment, high willingness to pay, strong East Africa and diaspora fit)
3. **NGO Management** (aligns with existing CONSENT relationship, donor-funded buyers have budget)
4. **HR and Payroll** (universal SME need, recurring pain point, easy upsell to existing clients)
5. **Clinic Management** (higher complexity but strong market, less competition than in Western markets)
6. **Property Management** (growing urban rental market, simpler to build, strong individual landlord segment)
7. **Church CRM** (large addressable market in East Africa, price-sensitive but high volume)
8. **E-Learning** (highest build complexity, but strong long-term revenue potential once others are live)

## Notes on Commercialisation

- All products should share a common billing and subscription layer so Aloflux can manage them from one admin interface.
- Priority markets are Uganda and East Africa for the near term, with US and UK diaspora as a secondary channel.
- Each product should have a 14-day free trial and self-serve onboarding to reduce sales friction.
- Localisation matters: statutory fields (NSSF, PAYE, Uganda Shillings) should be handled correctly from day one.
- Consider white-label licensing as an additional revenue stream for resellers and implementing partners.
